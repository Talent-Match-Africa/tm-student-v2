## Design Context

### Users

University students use this authenticated portal to discover jobs and internships, manage applications, connect with counselors, schedule appointments, access career resources, and maintain application-ready profiles. The interface must help them complete these tasks quickly and confidently across desktop and mobile devices.

### Brand Personality

Professional, supportive, and trustworthy. The experience should feel calm and precise while retaining the established Talent Match identity.

### Aesthetic Direction

Match the Talent Match administrator portal exactly for shared workspace patterns, measurements, typography, controls, spacing, responsive behavior, and interaction states. Use Outfit, the established `#49151A` brand color, a light interface, restrained surfaces, and the existing component system. Student-specific differences belong in content, permissions, routes, and actions—not in the shared visual language.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve exact administrator-portal parity for shared layouts and controls.
2. Keep student workflows task-focused and progressively disclose advanced filters.
3. Reuse shared primitives and tokens instead of introducing one-off styles.
4. Maintain accessible focus, reduced-motion, responsive, and touch behavior.
5. Protect student trust through clear states, safe actions, and concise language.
